Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Drag the diagonal pins from the tray at the bottom onto the grid, then tap the ball to release it. Every pin the ball hits turns it 90° and plays a note. Guide the ball to the marked exit to solve the level.
What are coins for?
You earn coins by solving levels. Spend them on hints: a hint reveals where one pin goes.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. There is no account or sign-in, and the game can be played offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No.
I’m stuck on a level. Can I start it over?
Yes. Tap the reset button in the top-right corner to put the level back to its starting state.
